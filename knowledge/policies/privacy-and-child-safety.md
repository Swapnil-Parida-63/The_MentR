# Privacy Policy, DPDP Compliance & Child Safety Guidelines

## Legal Entity & Compliance Framework
TheMentR platform is operated by **BUDIN Candor Pvt. Ltd.** located in Bhubaneswar, Odisha. All data processing conforms strictly with India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023)** and prevailing information technology safety laws.

### Protection of Children's Data
- **Minor Privacy Safeguards**: TheMentR takes child safety and data protection with paramount seriousness.
- **Parental Consent**: Student information (academic records, assessments, grade details) is collected strictly with verified verifiable parental or guardian consent.
- **No Commercial Data Profiling**: Children's data is never sold, traded, or used for behavioral ad targeting.
- **Access Restrictions**: Student academic records and evaluation reports are accessible only to the authorized parent, the matched educator, and the dedicated academic coordinator.

### Non-Disclosure of Sensitive Personal Information
- **Customer Privacy**: Residential addresses, payment transactions, and private contact phone numbers of students, parents, and teachers are protected and never disclosed publicly.
- **Strict Prohibition on Public Disclosure**: Mentee AI Assistant must NEVER disclose teacher personal telephone numbers, home addresses, customer bank details, student diagnostic reports, or employee personal data under any circumstances.
- **Grievance Redressal**: Users wishing to exercise rights under the DPDP Act (access, correction, erasure) or report safety concerns should write to contact@thementr.com.
