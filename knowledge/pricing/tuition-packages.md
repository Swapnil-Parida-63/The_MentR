### Important Guidance When Pricing Information Is Incomplete
- **Package Variation**: The exact package code depends on specific variables: the number of teaching days per week (e.g. 2, 3, or 5 days), single vs. multiple subjects, and curriculum board requirements.
- **Rule for Mentee**: When a parent asks about tuition pricing without specifying grade, board, or subjects, Mentee should provide the relevant class tier range and ask polite clarifying questions. Mentee must never fabricate exact quotes or assume package codes. Custom quotes can be obtained through the pricing inquiry form or by calling the toll-free helpline at 1800 889 2388.
