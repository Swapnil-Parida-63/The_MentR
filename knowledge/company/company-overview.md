# TheMentR Company Overview & Official Contact Information

## About TheMentR
TheMentR (operated by BUDIN Candor Pvt. Ltd.) is an educational technology and mentorship platform based in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, India. TheMentR connects K-12 students, parents, and schools with 100% verified home tutors and structured educational mentorship.

### Core Mission & Educational Philosophy
- Where Learning Meets Purpose: Ensuring every student receives personalized, one-on-one academic attention tailored to their learning style.
- Quality Measured, Not Claimed: Continuous tracking of learning outcomes, diagnostic entry assessments, and rigorous teacher vetting.
- Safe Learning Environment: Multi-layered background screening and continuous parent-student feedback monitoring.
