# TheMentR Home Tuition & 1-on-1 Mentorship Services

## 1-on-1 Personalized Home Tuition
TheMentR provides structured, one-on-one home tutoring and mentorship designed around the student's unique pace, board requirements, and learning gaps.

### Boards and Curriculums Supported
- CBSE (Central Board of Secondary Education)
- ICSE / ISC (Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations)
- IGCSE / International Boards
- Odisha State Board (BSE Odisha)
- CHSE Odisha (Council of Higher Secondary Education)
- SSVM (Shree Sathya Sai & Saraswati Vidya Mandir) Curriculums

### Key Service Features
- **In-Home Personal Guidance**: Distraction-free, focused learning in the safe environment of the student's home.
- **Curriculum-Aligned Lesson Plans**: Study schedules mapped directly to school term exams and board formats.
- **Conceptual Clarity over Rote Learning**: Deep emphasis on fundamental understanding, problem-solving, and answering techniques.
- **Regular Progress Tracking**: Monthly progress reviews, mock tests, and structured feedback shared directly with parents.
- **Student-Teacher Compatibility**: Teachers matched based on personality fit, pedagogy style, and board familiarity.

### How Tutor Matching Works
1. **Requirements Gathering**: Parent or student submits learning goals, grade, board, subject requirements, and preferred schedule.
2. **AVSAR Diagnostic Assessment**: A comprehensive 60–90 minute at-home evaluation identifies the student's learning style, strengths, and academic gaps.
3. **Targeted Match**: TheMentR matches the student with a 100% verified educator who specializes in that board and subject area within the student's locality.
4. **Structured 30-Day Goal Setting**: The assigned mentor sets measurable short-term academic milestones.
5. **Continuous Quality Monitoring & Reassignment**: If the match is not optimal, TheMentR provides a smooth review and tutor reassignment to guarantee uninterrupted learning.
