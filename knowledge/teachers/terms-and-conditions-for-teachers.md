### 3. TEACHER REGISTRATION AND ELIGIBILITY

#### 3.1 Minimum Eligibility Criteria
To register as a teacher on TheMentR, you must satisfy:

1. **Age and Citizenship:**
   - Be at least 18 years of age.
   - Be an Indian citizen or possess valid work authorization in India.
   - Have legal capacity to enter into binding contracts.

2. **Minimum Educational Qualifications:** *(Possession of mere educational qualifications does not guarantee service allocation)*
   - **Category A (Classes 0 to 3):** Minimum 10+2 from any recognized board.
   - **Category B (Classes 4 to 7):** Bachelor's degree (B.Sc., B.Com., B.A., etc.).
   - **Category C (Classes 8 to 10):** Bachelor's degree with subject specialization.
   - **Category D (Classes 8 to 10 - Individual Subjects):** Master's degree in relevant subject.
   - **Category E (Classes 11 & 12):** Master's degree or higher professional qualification.
   - **Category F (Subject Experts):** Advanced professional/post-graduate qualifications with minimum 5 years of verified teaching experience for competitive examinations.

3. **Additional Requirements:**
   - B.Ed. / M.Ed. / TET qualification for Classes 1 to 8 (preferable).
   - Must appear and qualify Expert Interview & Personality Test prior to Letter of Agreement (LoA) issuance.
   - Clean criminal record with zero pending criminal cases.
   - Physical and mental fitness to work with children.

#### 3.2 Prohibited Categories
You are ineligible if you:
- Have been convicted of any criminal offence, especially offences against children.
- Have been terminated from any educational institution for misconduct.
- Face active charges related to child safety or educational malpractice.
- Have been blacklisted by any educational authority.
- Suffer from any contagious disease or history of substance abuse affecting professional capability.

### 4. VERIFICATION AND BACKGROUND CHECKS

#### 4.1 Mandatory Verification Process
All teachers undergo rigorous multi-step screening:
- **Document Verification:** Government IDs (Aadhaar, PAN, Passport, Voter ID), educational degree certificates, marksheets, experience certificates, and mandatory e-mail, phone number & Aadhaar electronic verification.
- **Background Screening:** Police / third-party verification, past employer reference checks, social media screening, and professional conduct audits.
- **Skills Assessment:** Subject matter written/oral evaluation, demo lesson recording, language proficiency, and child safety awareness test.

#### 4.2 Ongoing Verification
- Annual mandatory re-verification of credentials.
- Periodic background check updates and continuous monitoring of criminal/legal status.

### 5. TEACHER CODE OF CONDUCT

#### 5.1 Professional Ethics and Standards
- **Teaching Excellence:** Prepare thoroughly with lesson plans, deliver engaging sessions, adhere to syllabus timelines, and provide regular progress reports to parents.
- **Professional Attire & Appearance:** Maintain high standards of personal hygiene, self-grooming, and formal professional attire during sessions. *(Prohibited during teaching sessions: round-neck t-shirts, torn jeans, shorts, tracksuits, etc.)*

#### 5.2 Interaction & Boundary Guidelines
- Treat all students with equal dignity without discrimination, harassment, or favoritism.
- Respect student and family privacy. Refrain from attending private student/family functions, religious rituals, or parties to avoid conflict of interest.
- Maintain absolute truth, transparency, and integrity in all platform communications.

### 6. CRIMINAL LAW COMPLIANCE & CHILD PROTECTION

#### 6.1 Strict Zero Tolerance
TheMentR enforces **absolute zero tolerance** for criminal activities and offences under:
- **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), 2023** (Sections 93-96 regarding child protection and endangerment).
- **Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012**.
- **Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Act, 2015**.
- **Right to Education (RTE) Act, 2009** (Prohibition of corporal punishment and mental harassment).

#### 6.2 Prohibited Direct Deals
Teachers are strictly barred from entering into any direct private financial deals or side agreements with parents/institutions that bypass TheMentR platform. Any unauthorized direct arrangement will lead to immediate termination, legal action, and forfeiture of accrued dues.

#### 6.3 Mandatory Reporting
Teachers must immediately report any observed criminal activity, child abuse, or safety threat on or off the platform to TheMentR administration and law enforcement authorities.

### 8. COMPENSATION AND PAYMENT TERMS

#### 8.1 Structured Pay-out System
- **No Capping on Earnings:** Remuneration is calculated per class hour based on availability, student loading, and category level (Categories A through F).
- **Extra Classes:** Half-hour extra sessions arranged with mutual parent-teacher consent are compensated separately on monthly payslips.
- **Mobile Allowance:** Additional mobile allowance up to ₹300/month (matching statutory Professional Tax deduction).
- **Accidental Insurance:** Group insurance coverage active from the issuance of the first monthly payslip.
- **Payment Cycle:** Monthly payout via direct bank transfer processed in the 3rd week of each succeeding month (on or before the 15th) for teachers who satisfactorily complete sessions up to the 10th of the month.
- **Digital Payslips:** Downloadable real-time earning statements available in the 'Earnings' section of the Teacher App.
