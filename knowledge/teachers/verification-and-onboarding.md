# Teacher Verification, Eligibility, Onboarding & Milestone Progression

## Teacher Eligibility Criteria
TheMentR maintains an inclusive, skill-oriented recruitment policy for educators.
- **Minimum Educational Qualifications**: Candidates must hold at least 10+2, ITI, or Diploma qualifications. Candidates holding higher degrees such as B.Sc., B.Ed., M.Ed., M.Sc., M.Tech, LLB, M.Com., BA, MA, B.Com., or PhD are eligible.
- **Experience Levels**: Both talented freshers and experienced teachers are welcome. All candidates are evaluated on actual teaching competence, communication skills, and subject clarity rather than paper degrees alone.

## 7-Stage Teacher Verification Process
To guarantee 100% safety and pedagogical excellence, every teacher on TheMentR must pass seven sequential verification stages:

1. **Stage 1: Application (Initial Vetting)**
   - Review of applicant's basic educational profile, teaching subjects, preferred localities, and background details.
2. **Stage 2: Profile Review (Credentials Check)**
   - Verification of educational certificates, mark sheets, degrees, and prior teaching history.
3. **Stage 3: Interview (Pedagogical Fit)**
   - Structured one-on-one discussion assessing communication clarity, empathy, teaching philosophy, and board familiarity.
4. **Stage 4: Demo Session (Live Teaching Assessment)**
   - Live simulated teaching session evaluated by academic experts on explanation skills, concept breaking, and student engagement.
5. **Stage 5: Identity Check (Security & Background Review)**
   - Government ID verification (Aadhaar/PAN), address confirmation, reference checks, and police background screening.
6. **Stage 6: Training (Playbook Alignment)**
   - Onboarding into TheMentR pedagogical standards, student empathy guidelines, monthly milestone tracking, and safety protocols.
7. **Stage 7: Approval (Ready to Teach)**
   - Award of TheMentR Verified Teacher badge, platform profile activation, and eligibility for student matching.

## Teacher Milestone Progression & Crown Badges
TheMentR publicly honors dedicated educators based on cumulative completed teaching hours:
- **Starter (0–99 hours)**: 🌱 MentR Starter. Beginning the teaching journey.
- **Bronze Crown (100–249 hours)**: 🥉 First major milestone. Public recognition on verified teacher directory.
- **Silver Crown (250–499 hours)**: 🥈 Consistent Mentor. Dedicated pedagogical consistency.
- **Gold Crown (500–749 hours)**: 🥇 Experienced Mentor. Recognized for transforming student outcomes.
- **Platinum Crown (750–999 hours)**: 💎 Elite Mentor. Academic dedication and leadership.
- **Diamond Crown (1,000–2,499 hours)**: 🔷 Master Mentor. Over 1,000 hours of master-level pedagogy.
- **Royal Crown (2,500–4,999 hours)**: 👑 Exceptional Contribution. Extraordinary foundational leadership.
- **MentR Legend (5,000+ hours)**: 🏆 Lifetime Educational Legacy. Unmatched lifetime dedication.
