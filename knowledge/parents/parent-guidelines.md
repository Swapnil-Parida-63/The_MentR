# Parent Guidelines, Code of Conduct & Consultation Workflow

## Guidelines for Parents
TheMentR partners closely with parents to ensure a safe, supportive, and productive home learning environment.

### Code of Conduct for Parents
- **Safe Learning Space**: Provide a quiet, well-lit, distraction-free environment for in-home tutoring sessions.
- **Punctuality & Scheduling**: Ensure the student is ready on time. Communicate scheduling adjustments in advance through official platform channels.
- **Respectful & Collaborative Interaction**: Treat teachers with professional dignity and respect. Tutors are academic partners, not domestic staff.
- **Direct Feedback & Concerns**: Any pedagogical concern, scheduling change, or tutor reassignment request must be routed through TheMentR customer support rather than unofficial private arrangements.
- **Child Safety Oversight**: An adult family member should always be present at home during in-home tuition sessions.

### Registration and Consultation Steps
1. **Submit Requirement**: Fill in student details (grade, board, subjects, location) on the website or via phone.
2. **Consultation & Assessment Visit**: Schedule an AVSAR diagnostic assessment visit (60–90 min) to evaluate learning style and establish a baseline.
3. **Teacher Assignment**: Receive a matched, background-verified tutor specializing in your board and subjects.
4. **Milestone Reviews**: Review monthly academic progress reports and discuss feedback directly with the mentor.

### Clarification on Demo / Trial Classes
- **Advisory**: Live demo sessions are conducted as part of the teacher vetting process (Stage 4). For prospective students and parents, TheMentR's verified approach begins with an in-home AVSAR diagnostic assessment to map learning gaps before tutor deployment.
- **Mentee Guidance**: Mentee should not make unverified, blanket promises that free trial classes are universally offered for every subject or grade. Instead, explain the diagnostic assessment visit and direct parents to the official support helpline (1800 889 2388) or consultation form.
