# Frequently Asked Questions (Parents, Students, Teachers & Institutions)

## Questions from Parents & Students

### How do students and parents join TheMentR?
Students or parents can register via the TheMentR website or mobile application by providing basic information: student name, grade/class, curriculum board, required subjects, locality, and preferred schedule. The team then connects to schedule an AVSAR diagnostic assessment.

### What is the fee range for home tuition through TheMentR?
TheMentR offers a transparent fee structure starting from **₹1,499 per month up to ₹9,999 per month** depending on class level (AP, BP, DP, EP), board, subjects, and weekly session frequency. All taxes and platform monitoring are included with zero hidden charges.

### How does TheMentR ensure teacher safety and quality?
Every teacher undergoes a strict 7-stage verification process: Application vetting, Profile review (credentials and degree authentication), Personal Interview for pedagogical fit, Live Demo Session, Government ID and Police Background Check, Pedagogical Playbook Training, and final Approval.

### What boards do TheMentR educators teach?
Educators cover CBSE, ICSE / ISC, IGCSE, Odisha State Board (BSE), and CHSE Odisha from Class 1 through Class 12, as well as competitive and Olympiad preparation.

## Questions from Teachers

### Who can apply to become a teacher with TheMentR?
Any candidate holding a minimum qualification of 10+2, ITI, or Diploma, or higher qualifications such as B.Sc., B.Ed., M.Ed., M.Sc., M.Tech, LLB, M.Com., BA, MA, B.Com., or PhD may apply. Recruitment is skill-based and welcoming of both freshers and experienced teachers.

### What extra benefits do verified teachers receive?
In addition to regular, guaranteed pay, verified teachers receive group insurance coverage, mobile phone allowance, performance reward bonuses, banking assistance (facilitation for personal loans and credit cards), verified badges, and official Experience Certificates.

## Questions from Educational Institutions

### What plans are available for educational institutions?
Institutions (schools, coaching centers, colleges) can purchase annual subscription plans at nominal rates for one academic year. This allows institutions to recruit verified visiting faculty through TheMentR on a per-class fee basis without paying additional placement commissions during the subscription year.
